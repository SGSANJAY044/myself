# hey, i'm sanjay.

I write code for a living. Lately I'm not sure "developer" is the right word for what I want to be.

The first time a C program I wrote printed a pyramid of stars, it felt like magic. Kind of dumb in hindsight, but that feeling stuck with me for years.

In college I chased internships after class and stayed up way too late doing it. Hackathons taught me you can build an idea overnight. They also taught me you can pitch something that already exists and still convince the judges it's innovative. Not proud of it. Learned a lot from it though.

Then I worked at a couple of mid-size startups. I learned how real teams work, and how a two-day task somehow becomes a two-week task. Honestly the architecture meetings were my favorite part.

Then AI showed up and the work got easy. Like, really easy. And somewhere in there I stopped enjoying coding.

Maybe I'm not supposed to be the best dev. Maybe I'm more of a builder. Maybe not. I don't know yet, and that's kind of the point.

## so what's this site?

I read [Alex West's story](https://alexwest.co/book-one) about running a bunch of small side projects until one of them turned into a real business. First thing in a while that got me excited the way that pyramid did.

So I'm trying it. Short projects. Ship them, see if anyone cares, figure out the business side I've always ignored. Pricing, getting users, all the stuff that isn't code.

This is where I'll put the [projects](projects/) and [write about](blog/) what worked, what didn't, and what I got wrong. Probably a lot of that last one.

If you're doing something similar, say hi on [GitHub](https://github.com/{{ site.github }}).
